# Contracts Zone

**Status:** Draft preparation surface  
**Work package:** `SKEL-P0-01`  
**Owner:** Engineering Authority

## Purpose

Draft platform-kernel contract shells and their traceability metadata.

## Preparation boundary

**Allowed now:** Open JSON Schema shells with mandatory draft control fields and no enforceable domain semantics.

**Prohibited now:** Stable contracts, normative approval claims, generated clients, migrations, or executable validation logic.

Nothing in this zone is an approved or stable dependency. Promotion requires the applicable implementation gate, exact-SHA review, required negative tests, and attributable Human Engineering Authority disposition.
