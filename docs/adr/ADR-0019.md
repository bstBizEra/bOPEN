# ADR-0019 — Identity audit is confidential, append-only, and never holds credentials

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Owner:** Data Authority
**Plan alias:** `ADR-P2-010` (BOPEN-IDP-001 §20)
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Membership & Enterprise Onboarding

## Context

Phase 2 generates the platform's most sensitive evidence: who authenticated, from which
provider, into which tenant, under what grant. This record is simultaneously a security
control, a compliance obligation and a privacy liability. It also sits directly in the path
of data-subject correction and erasure requests, which conflict with the integrity the audit
trail exists to provide.

## Decision drivers

- Audit must be trustworthy enough to rely on during an incident.
- Credentials in logs convert a log leak into a breach.
- Identity records must outlive Principals to prevent subject reassignment.
- Data-subject rights are legal obligations, not preferences.

## Options considered

1. **Log everything, redact later.** Convenient during development, and the redaction never
   happens. Rejected.
2. **Allowlist audit fields; mutate records on correction request.** Protects privacy and
   destroys the integrity property that makes audit useful. Rejected.
3. **Allowlist audit fields; append-only records; corrections as new records; tombstones
   retained.** Selected.

## Decision

Adopt option 3.

- **Classification.** Identity mappings, tenant memberships, roles, scopes, delegated grants
  and audit events are **Confidential Security Data**. External `subject`, `userName` and
  `email_normalized` are confidential personal data.
- **Prohibited from audit metadata and logs, without exception**: passwords, secrets, SAML
  assertions, OIDC authorization codes, access/ID/refresh/invitation/SCIM bearer tokens,
  complete broker payloads and unredacted personal profiles. This is enforced in code, not by
  convention, and violations fail closed rather than being silently dropped.
- **Append-only.** Audit events are never edited or deleted. A data-subject correction is a
  **new correction record** referencing the original; the original stands.
- **Retention**: security audit events **7 years** by default, overridable upward by tenant
  contract or downward only with a recorded exception. **Identity tombstones and
  non-reassignment records are retained indefinitely** — they hold identifiers, not profiles,
  and deleting them reintroduces the reassignment risk ADR-0011 closes.
- **Raw protocol payloads are not persisted.** Where short-lived capture is unavoidable for
  troubleshooting, it requires a separate approved retention rule with an expiry.
- Bounded metadata only: identifiers, policy versions, reason codes, outcomes,
  correlation/causation identifiers and timestamps.

## Consequences

- Erasure requests cannot empty the audit trail. The lawful basis for retaining security
  audit against an erasure request must be documented by the Data Authority — **outstanding
  work this ADR does not complete.**
- The 7-year default is a placeholder chosen to cover common financial-services retention.
  Tenant-specific residency and retention decisions are still required before production.
- Audit volume becomes a capacity planning input; the in-memory Phase 2 sink is not a
  retention mechanism and a durable append-only store is outstanding.
- Prohibited-field enforcement in code means a careless caller gets an error rather than a
  leak, at the cost of an occasional false positive on an innocuous field name.

## Security/data implications

- Closes INV-P2-018 (no credential, assertion or token in evidence).
- Append-only supports incident reconstruction and non-repudiation.
- Confidential classification drives access control on the audit store itself, which must not
  be readable by ordinary tenant administrators.

## Evidence

- `kernel_core/audit.py` — `PHASE2_EVENT_TYPES`, `PROHIBITED_METADATA_KEYS`,

- `tests/integration/test_phase2_membership_onboarding.py` — `P2-T066` prohibited-field scan,
  `P2-T067` fail-closed on contract violation.
- BOPEN-IDP-001 §15, §17.

## Supersedes / superseded by

Supersedes nothing. Constrains all Phase 2 audit emission.
