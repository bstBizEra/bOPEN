# ADR-0017 — Group-to-role mapping is versioned data owned jointly by tenant and security

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Owner:** Security Authority
**Plan alias:** `ADR-P2-008` (BOPEN-IDP-001 §20)
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Membership & Enterprise Onboarding

## Context

Enterprise directories carry group membership, and tenants reasonably expect their groups to
drive bOPEN roles. But a directory group is asserted by a system outside bOPEN's trust
boundary. If a group name can grant a role directly, whoever administers the tenant's
directory administers bOPEN's authorization.

## Decision drivers

- Tenants must be able to automate role assignment from their directory.
- A directory administrator must not be able to escalate beyond what bOPEN permits.
- Mapping changes are authorization changes and must be reviewable and reversible.
- Unmapped groups must be inert, not best-effort matched.

## Options considered

1. **Group name equals role name.** Zero configuration; directory administrator becomes a
   bOPEN authorization administrator. Rejected.
2. **Per-tenant free-form mapping table.** Tenant controls its own mapping, but nothing
   stops a tenant mapping a group to a platform-wide role. Insufficient alone.
3. **Versioned mapping table constrained by a security-owned role allowlist.** Selected.

## Decision

Adopt option 3 (D-P2-011).

- Mapping is **data, not code**, keyed `directory_id + group_external_id` and stamped with a
  `mapping_policy_version`.
- **Ownership is split**: the tenant authority proposes and maintains its mappings; the
  **Security Authority owns the allowlist of assignable roles**. A mapping whose target is
  outside the allowlist is invalid.
- **No group may map to a platform-wide, cross-tenant or support role.** Cross-tenant access
  is delegation only (ADR-0018).
- **Unmapped groups have no authorization effect.** They are ignored, not partially matched
  and not logged as errors.
- Inbound groups are **inputs to the mapping**, never grants. A mapping can never override
  inactive Principal, inactive Tenant, non-active Membership, explicit deny, expired
  delegation or tenant mismatch.
- Mapping application is audited with the policy version. A mapping change that reduces
  effective authorization triggers context invalidation.

## Consequences

- Onboarding a tenant now includes an explicit mapping step; groups do not "just work".
  That friction is the control.
- The role allowlist is a new security-owned artifact that does not yet exist and must be
  authored before group mapping is enabled in any environment.
- Versioning lets an authorization change be attributed to a specific mapping revision during
  incident review.
- Bulk directory reorganisations may require a coordinated mapping revision; without one,
  effective authorization silently narrows to unmapped-and-inert, which is the safe direction.

## Security/data implications

- Closes the forged-role/group claim threat (BOPEN-IDP-001 §16).
- Keeps the broker firmly outside the authorization decision (ADR-0010).
- Group identifiers are confidential directory data and are not carried in tokens.

## Evidence

- `platform_kernel/idp_bridge.py` — `GroupRoleMapping`, `resolve_mapping`,
  `_scim_group_changed`.
- `tests/unit/test_idp_bridge.py` — `P2-T050` mapped, `P2-T051` unmapped has no effect.
- BOPEN-IDP-001 §8.3, §10.4; ADR-0008 (authorization separate from entitlement).
