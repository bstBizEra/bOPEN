# ADR-0018 — Delegated access is bounded, approved by a second person, and never renews

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Owner:** Security Authority
**Plan alias:** `ADR-P2-009` (BOPEN-IDP-001 §20)
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Membership & Enterprise Onboarding

## Context

Support engineers need to enter a tenant to diagnose faults, and partners need scoped access
to tenants they serve. The usual outcome is a standing support role with broad rights that
nobody revokes. That role becomes the most valuable credential in the platform.

## Decision drivers

- Cross-tenant access must be exceptional, attributable and time-boxed.
- Expiry must be automatic; anything requiring manual cleanup will not be cleaned up.
- A single compromised or careless insider must not be able to self-authorize entry.
- Customers will eventually need to see who entered their tenant and why.

## Options considered

1. **Standing support role.** Operationally easy, permanently dangerous. Rejected.
2. **Time-boxed self-service grants.** Bounded in time but self-authorized; one compromised
   support account grants itself access. Rejected.
3. **Time-boxed grants with mandatory second-person approval and hard ceilings.** Selected.
4. **Customer-approved access only.** Strongest privacy posture; unworkable for
   customer-reported incidents outside business hours. Deferred as a per-tenant option.

## Decision

Adopt option 3.

- Grants are **tenant-specific, role-bounded, scope-bounded and time-bounded**. There is no
  wildcard tenant, role or scope, and the check is not configurable off (INV-P2-015).
- **Separation of duties is mandatory**: the creator may not approve. Approval and activation
  are distinct steps, and a grant that was never approved cannot activate.
- **Maximum durations**: support **8 hours** (D-P2-012); partner bounded by contract with a
  hard ceiling of **90 days** (D-P2-013). Durations cannot be extended silently — a longer
  need is a new grant with a new approval.
- **Support grants require a case or ticket reference.** Partner grants require a contract
  reference.
- **Expiry is automatic and never auto-renews.** Revocation is immediate for new issuance and
  invalidates active delegated contexts through the ADR-0014 obligation path (D-P2-014).
- Every delegated context carries a **`dgr` claim** resolving to an active, unexpired grant,
  and every delegated action records the grant reference in audit evidence.

## Consequences

- Support cannot act instantly and alone. An approver must be reachable; staffing an approval
  rota is an operational prerequisite, not an afterthought.
- The 90-day partner ceiling is a placeholder pending commercial input. It is a ceiling, not a
  default, and partner agreements may require shorter.
- Step-up authentication for sensitive delegated operations is specified in BOPEN-IDP-001
  §14.2 but **not implemented**; it needs its own decision.
- Customer-visible reporting of delegated access is a product requirement recorded here and
  deferred to a later phase.

## Security/data implications

- Removes the standing cross-tenant privilege class entirely.
- Bounds insider and account-compromise blast radius to one tenant, one scope set and one
  time window.
- `dgr` in audit makes every delegated action attributable to an approved grant.
- Grant reason codes and case references are confidential security data (ADR-0019).
