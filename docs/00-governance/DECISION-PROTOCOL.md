# Decision Protocol

Every material decision records:

- decision ID and title;
- status: proposed, accepted, rejected, superseded;
- context and problem;
- options considered;
- decision and rationale;
- security, tenancy, data, cost and operational implications;
- affected modules/contracts;
- owner, reviewers and approver;
- effective date;
- evidence;
- review or expiry date.

Use `templates/ADR.md`.
