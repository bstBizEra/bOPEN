# Operating Cadence

## Daily

- bounded maker sessions;
- automated checks;
- evidence update;
- blocker escalation.

## Weekly

- Monday authorization and worktree allocation;
- Wednesday architecture/security checkpoint for material issues;
- Friday checker review, integration decision and learning disposition.

## Monthly

- roadmap and KPI review;
- ADR/technology review;
- tenant-isolation control review;
- dependency and supply-chain review;
- skills evaluation;
- risk and exception expiry review;
- backup/restore evidence review.
