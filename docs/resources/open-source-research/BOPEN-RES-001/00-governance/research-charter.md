# Research Charter

## Mandate

Create defensible architecture knowledge for bOPEN by studying open-source platforms under controlled provenance, evidence and clean-room rules.

## Success definition

The research succeeds when reviewers can trace every proposed bOPEN requirement back to classified evidence, understand which parts are observation versus inference, and confirm that the implementation team did not receive upstream source as an implementation input.

## Principles

1. Evidence before architecture assertion.
2. Architecture before implementation.
3. Membership is a relationship, not a copied user.
4. Authorization, entitlement and feature rollout are separate controls.
5. Upstream naming is not automatically bOPEN terminology.
6. Upstream convenience is not automatically a bOPEN standard.
7. Security and license obligations are design inputs.
8. Reproducibility is mandatory.
9. Negative findings are valid research outputs.
10. The research clone is disposable; the evidence pack is durable.
