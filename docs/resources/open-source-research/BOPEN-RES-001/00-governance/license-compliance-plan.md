# License Compliance Plan

## Baseline

The pinned BoxyHQ repository contains the Apache License 2.0 text. This package records that observation but does not provide legal advice.

## Mandatory controls

1. Preserve the upstream `LICENSE`, notices and attribution in the research clone.
2. Do not remove copyright or license headers.
3. Record the exact commit and any local modifications.
4. Keep research-clone patches separate from bOPEN production changes.
5. Review transitive dependencies and their licenses before redistribution.
6. Obtain legal review before distributing a modified BoxyHQ derivative.
7. Do not use BoxyHQ or Ory names/logos as bOPEN branding.
8. Treat SAML Jackson/Ory Polis, Retraced, Svix, Stripe and other integrations as separate dependency/license surfaces.

## Decision paths

- **Study only:** permitted subject to normal license preservation and organizational policy.
- **Run internally:** security and dependency review required.
- **Reuse source:** requires explicit legal and architecture approval; not authorized by BOPEN-RES-001.
- **Independent implementation:** preferred bOPEN path, using clean-room specifications.
