# Observation, Inference and Decision Standard

Every analysis record shall use three explicit sections.

## Observation

A factual statement directly supported by a pinned source or reproduced behavior.

## Inference

A reasoned interpretation about architectural intent, consequence or bOPEN relevance. It must not be presented as upstream fact.

## Decision

An approved bOPEN choice, including rationale, alternatives and consequences. Only authorized reviewers may promote an inference to a decision.

## Prohibited phrasing

Avoid statements such as “BoxyHQ proves bOPEN should…” unless the statement has passed architecture review. Use “BoxyHQ demonstrates…” for observations and “bOPEN may…” for proposals.
