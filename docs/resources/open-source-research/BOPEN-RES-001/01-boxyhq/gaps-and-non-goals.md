# BoxyHQ Gaps and Non-Goals

## Confirmed/expected gaps against bOPEN

- generic principal hierarchy;
- party/person/organization business foundation;
- tenant state machine;
- tenant isolation profile;
- organization graph and legal entity model;
- separate role assignment domain;
- conditional and relationship authorization;
- delegated support access;
- generalized entitlement engine;
- usage metering;
- module/capability registry;
- workflow/event runtime;
- agent principal and tool grants;
- multi-industry packages;
- sovereign/dedicated deployment model.

## Non-goals for the BoxyHQ lab

- proving BoxyHQ production readiness for bOPEN;
- benchmarking all SaaS starters;
- replacing future Basejump, Logto, Frappe, OpenFGA or OpenMeter studies;
- converting BoxyHQ into bOPEN;
- standardizing bOPEN on Next.js, Prisma, NextAuth, Svix, Retraced or Stripe.
