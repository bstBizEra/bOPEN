## Question

May two named operators execute the pinned BoxyHQ target against isolated synthetic services to gather the missing E3/E4 identity, tenant/membership, and invitation lifecycle evidence?

## Options

1. Keep execution closed and retain G3 as open.
2. Approve a time-bounded, path-bounded, exact-hash execution authorization under the G3 design contract.
3. Permit ad hoc local execution without an exact authorization envelope.

## Recommendation

Select option 2 only after Architecture, Security, License, ENGIN, and independent REV owners are named and the execution envelope resolves exact dependency/image digests, allowed internal networks, synthetic fixtures, retention duration, evidence paths, cleanup targets, start time, and expiry time. Until then, option 1 remains effective.

Option 3 is rejected because it cannot provide reproducible authority, isolation, provenance, or cleanup controls.

## Required approval record

Approval must bind:

- upstream repository and exact commit `abc9b686823cbfb4973c79bc36fea37a3244be6c`;
- the reviewed G3 design-contract SHA-256 and validator SHA-256;
- named maker and independent checker operators;
- exact external workspace/evidence roots and permitted service/network boundaries;
- immutable dependency, tool, and image identities;
- synthetic-data declaration, secret-scan controls, retention deadline, and rollback/cleanup procedure;
- authorization issue, start, expiry, approver, and revocation fields.

## Current decision

**NO-GO for runtime execution.** This proposed record authorizes nothing by itself. Design validation may proceed, but no upstream process, container, database, network interception, or runtime evidence collection may start until a separate effective approval satisfies every required field.

## Impact

If approved and executed, the study may produce research evidence for a later G3 decision. A successful run still does not itself pass G3, authorize clean-room handoff, or permit production kernel implementation.
