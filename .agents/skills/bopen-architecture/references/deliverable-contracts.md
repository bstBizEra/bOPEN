# Deliverable Contracts

## Research report

Purpose: establish current facts, compare credible patterns, and derive bOPEN implications.

Required sections:

- question, scope, and date;
- source-selection method and freshness;
- observed facts with citations;
- comparison matrix;
- bOPEN implications and non-transferable assumptions;
- recommendation, risks, and unresolved issues;
- proposed successor artifacts.

## Architecture design

Purpose: define an implementable target system boundary and contracts.

Required sections:

- context, goals, non-goals, and constraints;
- principles and invariants;
- component and ownership model;
- principal/tenant/context/authorization flow;
- data model, state machines, APIs, and events;
- security, tenancy, operational, and supply-chain controls;
- alternatives and ADRs;
- implementation sequencing and verification;
- migration, rollback, and exit gates.

## ADR

Purpose: record one material decision.

Required sections: status, context, drivers, decision, options, rationale, consequences, controls, evidence, and supersession rules.

## Gap analysis

Purpose: compare current state with an approved target.

Each gap requires severity, affected control, current evidence, target requirement, remediation, owner, dependency, due date, and closure evidence.

## Implementation control package

Purpose: turn approved architecture into governed work.

Required sections:

- entry gates and technology freeze;
- work packages, dependencies, and RACI;
- acceptance criteria and mandatory evidence;
- verification levels and negative-test catalogue;
- supply-chain, recovery, and operational controls;
- exit gates and final conformance verdict.

## Conformance review

Purpose: issue a bounded verdict against identified controls.

Required sections:

- reviewed artifact/revision and scope;
- controls and evidence examined;
- findings by severity;
- tenant-isolation and authorization verdict;
- evidence-integrity verdict;
- conditions and blocking items;
- final disposition and approvers.
