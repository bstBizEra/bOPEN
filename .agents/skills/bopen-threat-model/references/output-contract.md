# Output Contract

- Architecture and data-flow diagrams
- STRIDE-A inventory
- Prioritized findings and mitigations
- Incremental change report

## Verdicts

- `pass`: mandatory controls evidenced.
- `conditional-pass`: bounded non-critical actions remain with named authority.
- `fail`: mandatory or non-waivable control failed.
- `decision-required`: authority evidence or architecture choice is missing.
