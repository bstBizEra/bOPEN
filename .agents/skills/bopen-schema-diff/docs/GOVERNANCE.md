# Governance

The package is a validated internal candidate. It is not approved, signed, published, or production-authorized.

Required before approval:

- independent database architecture review;
- independent tenant-isolation and RLS review;
- model-level activation and outcome evaluation;
- evaluation against the real bOPEN migration stack;
- approved Atlas and pg-schema-diff versions;
- license review;
- signed provenance and release artifact;
- registry publication decision.

The maker of a migration may not be its sole approver. The skill may not approve its own findings or execution.
